ClauseGuard AI is a contract-intelligence application that analyzes agreements, identifies important clauses, highlights context conflicts, and helps compare contract options.
Upload a contract, describe your situation and priorities, and the analysis engine extracts clauses, categorizes them, calculates risk signals and connects important findings to contract evidence.
This project supports PDF, TXT and MD files. DOCX can be added to the parser as an extension when needed.
In the local deployment, your documents are processed by the application running on your machine. Production deployments should add authentication, encrypted storage and a documented retention policy.
